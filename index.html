Our story

We came for the game.
We stayed for the people.

Palmetto Mahjong Company was born from a love of the rhythm, strategy, and connection found around a Mahjong table.

We believe the best way to learn is among friends, with plenty of encouragement and a little room to laugh. Our goal is to welcome new players into the game and give seasoned players a lovely reason to gather.

Good to know

Before we gather.

Do we need our own Mahjong sets?

No. We can bring the tiles, racks, cards, and teaching materials needed for your lesson or event.

How many people can attend?

Lessons work beautifully with four to eight players. Larger hosted events can be customized to your group and venue.

Where do you host events?

We come to private homes, clubs, offices, and event venues. Share your location in the inquiry form and we will confirm availability.
