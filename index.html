Meet the founder

Jenna Sisk,
aka MahJenna.

Jenna has a passion for people, thoughtful hospitality, and the kind of connection that happens when everyone has a seat at the table.

After entering the event planning industry in 2019, Jenna joined the nonprofit world in 2023 and went on to host hundreds of community events. Along the way, she discovered how much a welcoming gathering can mean to individuals, communities, and companies alike.

Jenna first sought out Mahjong as a social outlet and an intellectual challenge. She was quickly mesmerized by the beautiful tiles, charming tablescapes, and the game’s rare ability to bring people together. Palmetto Mahjong Company grew from her desire to share all three with anyone willing to learn.

Good to know

Before we gather.

Do we need our own Mahjong sets?

No. We can bring the tiles, racks, cards, and teaching materials needed for your lesson or event.

How many people can attend?

Lessons work beautifully with four to eight players. Larger hosted events can be customized to your group and venue.

Where do you host events?

We come to private homes, clubs, offices, and event venues. Share your location in the inquiry form and we will confirm availability.
